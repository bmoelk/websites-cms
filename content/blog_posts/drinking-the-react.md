Day 2 and beyond of Chain React

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1000/1*fyiSJh7REjy2TwxrA1sihg.jpeg)

One of the things I love about going to ***great*** conferences is that they expand my mind. I learn a bunch, but most of all, I begin to think differently.

I’ve had a few days to reflect on Chain React, here are my thoughts:

1. Mobile first strategy isn’t limited to a responsive web design. It’s about getting a compelling app in the hands of people using your services, or in the case of ad revenue, being your product :)
2. Microsoft is doing a really good job at supporting mobile developers with [vs.code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) and [Visual Studio Mobile Center](https://mobile.azure.com). I’m in.
3. [GraphQL](http://graphql.org/) is for real. REST based CRUD is no longer good enough.
4. It’s fun to hear a speaker casually say “push it to [now](https://zeit.co/now)” and know that I’m with my peeps.
5. Facebook’s open source technology successfully powers applications more like what most devs/businesses build vs. search, gmail, maps, etc.
6. Mobile applications are hard no matter what technology you choose. Professional quality is hard to achieve in any target, but mobile has it’s own unique challenges.

Thanks [InfiniteRed](https://infinite.red/). I had a great time. Now off to build out [drawdown.pro ](https://drawdown.pro)w/react-native.
